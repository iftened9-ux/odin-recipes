Guacamole Recipe

A fresh, simple guacamole, perfect as a dip or a topping.

Home

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl
  2. Mash the avocado with a fork to your preferred consistency (chunky or smooth)
  3. Add the chopped onion, tomato, garlic, and cilantro
  4. Squeeze in the lime juice and mix well
  5. Season with salt to taste
  6. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, or cover tightly and refrigerate to prevent browning
