Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

A classic Italian pasta dish with just a handful of ingredients, ready in 15 minutes.

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt, to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes, and cook gently until the garlic is golden (do not let it burn)
  4. Reserve about 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti
  5. Add the drained spaghetti to the pan with the garlic oil, tossing to coat. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water if needed
  6. Stir in the chopped parsley and season with salt
  7. Serve immediately, topped with Parmesan if desired
